A little about me

Design meets technology.

I'm pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering at BML Munjal University. My technical background helps me understand how digital products work, while my interest in UI/UX pushes me to think about how people experience them.

I am interested in information architecture, visual hierarchy, intuitive navigation, and creating interfaces that help users complete tasks with less friction.

Zephyr

A campus navigation concept designed to help students and visitors find university buildings and facilities more easily.

  • Organized campus categories and facilities
  • Accessible information about destinations
  • A straightforward path from search to directions
